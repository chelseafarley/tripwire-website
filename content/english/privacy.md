## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tripwire Tech** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in **Maths For Kids** and other apps that link to this page.

App-specific policies also exist for some products:

- [Sports journal apps privacy policy](/sports-journal-privacy-policy/) (Curling Diary, Lawn Bowls Diary, Speed Puzzling Diary)
- [Revenue Tap privacy policy](/revenue-tap-privacy-policy/)

If you have questions, contact **chelsea@tripwiretech.com**.

## Maths For Kids

Maths For Kids is a maths learning app for children. Play does **not** require creating an account with Tripwire Tech.

Information you or a parent create in the app (for example child profiles and progress) is stored **locally on your device**. We do not operate a Maths For Kids cloud account for that content.

The App does **not** sell personal information.

## Information We Collect

Unless an app-specific policy says otherwise:

- The apps that use this page do **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal information to Tripwire Tech for advertising profiles.
- Information you enter is stored **locally on your device** (for example in an on-device database).

## Local Storage

Data remains on your device unless you choose to delete the App or clear its data. The developer does not have access to on-device diary or learning progress unless you deliberately send it (for example by email for support).

## Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

Unless an app-specific policy says otherwise, apps covered by this page:

- Do not use analytics for marketing profiling
- Do not use advertising networks (unless stated in that app’s store listing or a dedicated privacy page)
- Do not use tracking technologies to follow you across other companies’ apps for advertising
- Do not share your on-device learning or journal content with third parties for their marketing

## Permissions

The App only requests permissions required for its functionality. If additional permissions are added in future releases, this policy will be updated.

## Children's Privacy

Maths For Kids is designed for young learners with parent or carer guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on Tripwire Tech servers. Profile and progress data stay on the device.

## Data Security

Because your data is stored only on your device by default, you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date.
