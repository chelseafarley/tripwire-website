**Revenue Tap** is a **local-first publisher analytics app** made by **Tripwire Tech**. It puts AdMob, AdSense, Google Play, App Store Connect, and Google Analytics on one dashboard so you can review ads, store proceeds, installs, and usage without jumping between consoles.

## What problem it solves

Indie publishers often check five consoles to answer a simple question: how is revenue doing this week? Revenue Tap is a **publisher analytics dashboard** that connects the accounts you already use and keeps credentials and reports **on your device**.

## Who it is for

Independent app and web publishers who want:

- An **AdMob / AdSense earnings** view alongside store proceeds
- Install and usage context from Play and Analytics
- Targets, currency conversion, and exports — without a Tripwire cloud account

## Key features

### One dashboard for publishers

Home shows estimated earnings, ad revenue, store proceeds, installs, and active users for the range you pick. Filter by app, group Android and iOS listings, convert amounts into your currency, and tap a metric to see where it came from.

Set a target and check whether your current pace is likely to get you there. Export CSV or PDF when you need a snapshot to share or file away.

### What you can connect

- Google AdMob and AdSense (read-only)
- Google Analytics 4
- Google Play reporting bucket (installs and earnings)
- App Store Connect (sales and proceeds)

Totals follow each source’s delay. Play and Apple figures can lag until reports land. Store proceeds are converted from the currency you were paid in — Apple’s own payout estimate can differ because of later FX and withholding.

## Platforms

Revenue Tap is built for **iOS and Android** on phone and tablet. App Store and Google Play links will be added when listings are live.

## Privacy and advertising

Credentials stay in on-device secure storage. Reporting data stays local. The app is free and funded by ads (Google Mobile Ads). Full details: [Revenue Tap privacy policy](/revenue-tap-privacy-policy/).

Revenue Tap is a reporting app, not a bank or payout tool. Delete all local data anytime in Settings.
