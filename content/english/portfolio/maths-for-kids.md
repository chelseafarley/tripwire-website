**Maths For Kids** is a **maths games and practice app for children aged about 5 to 8**, made by **Tripwire Tech**. It helps young learners build real number confidence through short, playful games — not worksheets.

## What problem it solves

Early maths practice often feels like a test: typing answers, dense worksheets, and one-size-fits-all drills. Maths For Kids gives children a calmer way in — visual games, Learn and Explore modes before questions, and help when they get stuck — while parents get profiles and progress they can act on.

## Who it is for

Families and carers supporting:

- Early primary **maths practice for kids ages 5–8**
- After-school fluency without high-pressure testing
- Children who need a gentler, more visual way into numbers

## Key features

### Built for how children learn

- **Learn and Explore modes** — space to experiment with a concept before answering questions
- **Low-stimulation visual games** — clear, friendly visuals and simple interactions
- **Help when they get stuck** — child-friendly explanations on incorrect answers so learners can self-correct
- **Grow with every module** — combine earlier skills with new topics so challenge stays achievable

### Everything parents need

- **Multiple child profiles** — track each child’s progress individually
- **Progress you can act on** — strengths and areas for improvement per learner
- **Many games, many levels** — Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert difficulty

## What your child can practise

### Counting and number sense

Counting, comparing numbers, number lines, before and after, and building target numbers.

### Place value

Hundreds, tens and ones, expanded numbers, and digit value.

### Addition and subtraction

Facts, missing numbers, number-line jumps, story problems, and finding the difference.

### Multiplication and division

Equal groups, arrays, times tables, sharing, remainders, and missing factors.

### Fractions, decimals and percents

Pizza fractions, equivalent fractions, fractions of a group, decimals, and percent practice.

### Patterns

Number patterns, shape sequences, missing pieces, and pattern rules.

### Geometry

2D and 3D shapes, symmetry, grids and coordinates, perimeter, area, volume, and angles.

### Data

Sorting, tally marks, bar charts, pie charts, line charts, and data questions.

### Time

Analogue clocks, setting the time, before and after, elapsed time, schedules, and am/pm.

### Measurement and money

Length, mass, capacity, temperature, calendars, unit conversions, and counting money.

### Today’s Challenge

A mixed set of questions that pulls skills together across games — great for review and stretch.
