**50 First Foods** is a **baby first foods tracker and starting solids checklist** made by **Tripwire Tech**. It gives parents a simple goal: help your baby explore **50 different foods**, while also introducing flavours, textures, and common allergens.

## What problem it solves

Starting solids is exciting — and easy to lose track of. Which foods has your baby tried? What did they enjoy? Which allergens have you introduced? What should you try next? **50 First Foods** turns that mental load into a calm, on-device checklist and food journal.

## Who it is for

Parents and carers who want:

- A practical **baby food checklist** for variety
- An **allergen tracker** alongside first foods
- Support for **baby-led weaning**, purées, or a mix
- Offline tracking with **no account required**

## Key features

### Track 50 first foods

A curated collection of **50 first foods** gives you a practical starting point. For each attempt you can record the date, whether they enjoyed it, how the food was prepared, notes, and pairings that worked well.

### Explore 20 flavours

The app includes **20 flavour ideas** so you can think beyond plain ingredients — mild herbs, spices, and combinations appropriate for your baby’s stage.

### Keep track of common allergens

Separately track **10 common allergens** alongside the main food list, with notes on individual attempts. Always prepare foods in a form appropriate for your baby’s age and eating abilities.

### Favourites and “Needs More Exposure”

Record foods as Enjoyed, Neutral, or Disliked. Foods that may benefit from another try appear in **Needs More Exposure**, so one messy attempt doesn’t mean you forget that food forever.

### Weekly meal planner and prepped foods

Plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner slots for the week, and keep track of **batch-prepared** baby food so you can see what’s already ready.

### Add your own foods

The journey doesn’t stop at food number 50. Add custom foods, family recipes, cultural foods, and favourite combinations.

### Private and offline

Works offline and stores information on your device — no account, no cloud sync required, and no personal data collection by the app for tracking.
